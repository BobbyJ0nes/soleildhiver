À Rose

C'est 14:42...

I'm not going to try and write poetry for you in French. I thought about it, but it's like you wrote. I think we speak the same language.

It's interesting to me how things have unfolded. In all honesty it wasn't part of my plan to care for someone at this point in time. I wish I could give you the bouquet you deserve, but that's not me right now — best I can offer is a single Rose. I thought about how you told me that it was your childhood dream to become a poet, and after reading your poetry, I can see why. Amongst other things, I like the way you see the world. I want to say that I'm proud of you, but I wrestle whether I have the authority to. I like the way you look at the ground when you smile sometimes. And how you edge closer to me when we smoke. I never see your feet move — maybe you really could fly as a child. I like when you keep asking me annoying questions to poke holes in my idea, and when a grin creeps through your facade. I like how you let your thoughts sit and linger, and pick them back up off the floor and dust them off, before neatly presenting them on the shelf.

There are many things I like about you, but you don't need to hear those. I like how you don't need to hear anything from me — but like to anyways. Your soul is pretty and your heart seems pure, I pray you'll get what you need in this life.

I hope you have the best year.
